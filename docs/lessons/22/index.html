System prompts: separate what stays from what changes

A prompt that repeats its rules every turn pays the same tax twice: once to write the rules, once to have the model re-read them alongside whatever's actually new.

One change, same AI.

Your prompt
Always cite your sources. Never use exclamation marks. Keep answers under 200 words. Summarise your task.
A stronger version
System prompt: Always cite your sources. Never use exclamation marks. Keep answers under 200 words.
Per-turn ask: Summarise your task.

Here is the test: read your system prompt line by line and ask, would this be false on some future turn?
