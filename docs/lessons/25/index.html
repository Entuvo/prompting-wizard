Before generating: your task will be judged against three checks — every claim traces to a source, no paragraph exceeds five sentences, and the conclusion appears in the first paragraph. Now produce your task and score it against those three checks, unchanged.

Here is the test: show your criteria to someone who hasn't seen the output. Could they score it without asking you what you meant?
