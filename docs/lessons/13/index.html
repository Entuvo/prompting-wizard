Particle: phrasal precision

Say "look over" when you meant "look up" and you get a skim where you wanted a lookup — wrong operation, nothing looked broken.

One change, same AI.

Your prompt
Look over the session times for the staff training day in the supplied timetable.
A stronger version
Look up the session times for the staff training day in the supplied timetable.

Here is the test: swap the particle for a plausible alternative, then swap the whole phrasal verb for a plain verb.
