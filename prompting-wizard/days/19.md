# Day 19 — Reasoning scaffolds: name the intermediates

## Concept

"Think step by step about {{TASK}}" asks for reasoning without saying what the steps are. The model produces steps — some invented, in whatever order looks plausible — and you can't tell whether the ones that mattered actually appeared, because you never named them.

A scaffold names the real intermediates: things that must exist before the answer can be trusted. Not "think it through" but "first list your assumptions, then check each against what's given, then answer." Each named step can be inspected on its own, catching a wrong answer before it's delivered.

The scaffold has to gate the answer too, not just precede it. "Do not recommend until every assumption is checked" stops the model writing intermediates as decoration after it's already decided — cheap at faking depth. Name the intermediates, and forbid answering until they exist.

Here is the test: read a "think step by step" prompt of yours and ask what must exist before its answer is trustworthy. If you can't name it, the model can't either — it names its own, and you'll never know which one it skipped.

## Before / After

**Before**

> Think step by step about {{TASK}}.

**After**

> For {{TASK}}: first list every assumption you're making that isn't stated in the material. Second, check each assumption against what's actually given, and mark it confirmed or unconfirmed. Do not give an answer until every assumption is marked. Then answer, citing which assumptions it depends on.

"Step by step" left the steps to the model's judgement. The rewrite names two specific intermediates — the assumption list, the confirmation pass — and gates the answer on both existing first.

## Exercise

Pick one of your recurring tasks. Write a single prompt for it.

### Novice

Write a "think step by step" prompt for {{TASK}}, then rewrite it naming the specific intermediates the answer depends on, and state what the answer must not do until they exist.

### Working

Write a prompt for {{TASK}} that names the intermediates required before the answer, with the answer explicitly gated on their existing first.

### Advanced

Write a prompt for {{TASK}} that names the intermediates and states what the answer must not do until they exist — the gate has to be enforceable, not just a suggestion of order.
